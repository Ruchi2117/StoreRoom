# Public demonstration image

`shelf.jpg` is an unchanged copy of `IMG_20181218_170247.jpg` from HoloSelecta V1, used in StoreRoom's existing validation split. It is not from the protected test set or a private field collection.

Authors: Klaus Fuchs, Tobias Grundmann, Mirella Haldimann, Elgar Fleisch.

Source: [HoloSelecta V1](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/gz39ggf35n/1), DOI 10.17632/gz39ggf35n.1.
License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Related publication: Fuchs et al., *Supporting food choices in the Internet of People: Automatic detection of diet-related activities and display of real-time interventions via mixed reality headsets*, 2020, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.future.2020.07.014).

Screenshots in `src/web/screenshots/` show this image displayed/rescaled by the real application, sometimes with the model's box overlays. The underlying photo was not retouched. Predictions and reviewed demonstration quantities are not an independent accuracy measurement or evidence of a real participating shop's stock.
